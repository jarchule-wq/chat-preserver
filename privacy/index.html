Chat Preserver

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

Chat Preserver collects nothing. It runs entirely in your browser, saves your ChatGPT and Claude chats only to a folder you choose on your computer, and never sends your chats or files to us or to anyone else. The only service it talks to besides ChatGPT and Claude is ExtensionPay, our payment service, to know whether your copy has Pro. That check carries a random ID, never your chats.

  • No sign-up to use it, and no server of ours.
  • No analytics, tracking, ads or cookies.
  • Your chats and files never leave your computer, except for the requests your browser already makes to ChatGPT or Claude to load them.

What it accesses and why

Chat Preserver works on ChatGPT or Claude only when you click its button, or at the scheduled backup time you set yourself. It reads no other website, apart from ExtensionPay's own payment pages (below).

It has standing access only to extensionpay.com, chatgpt.com, claude.ai and Claude's frame address above. It reads chatgpt.com or claude.ai only when you click its button or at the backup time you set, and never reads other websites, other tabs or your browsing history.

While saving, it reads from ChatGPT or Claude, through your own signed-in session:

  • the messages of the chats you chose to save (one chat, a project, or every chat in your account for a whole-account backup);
  • the files in them: your uploads, images, files the AI created, and project files;
  • for Find a file, the names, sizes and dates of the files in your account, used only to show you the list;
  • your session details, used only in memory to make those requests. They are never written to disk or sent anywhere else.

Where your data goes

Everything Chat Preserver saves goes to one place: the folder you pick on your own computer.

  • Saved files: the transcript, the reader page, every recovered file, a run report listing what was saved and checked, and for account backups an index page and a small state file recording what has been saved so later backups can skip it. Nothing else is kept.
  • Kept in your browser: your backup schedule and choices, and, so a scheduled backup can write to it, your browser's permission for the backup folder you picked. These stay on this computer.
  • Network requests: the extension talks to ChatGPT (chatgpt.com and the file links ChatGPT itself provides) and Claude (claude.ai and Claude's own frame and file addresses) to save your chats. It never contacts a server of ours.
  • Pro check: when Chat Preserver is installed, ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) gives this copy a random ID, which is stored inside the extension. When you click the toolbar button, the extension sends that ID to ExtensionPay to ask whether this copy has Pro. The ID isn't tied to you unless you buy Pro or restore a purchase, which links it to the email you enter. If ExtensionPay can't be reached, a copy confirmed as Pro in the last 30 days keeps working.
  • Offline reader: the reader page loads nothing from the internet. It works with the network switched off.
  • Not saved: sign-in tokens, cookies and the sites' temporary download links are used only while saving and are never written into your folder.

The saved folder is yours. We can't see it, change it, or recover it if it's lost. You can delete it at any time like any other folder.

What we never do

  • Collect, store or see your conversations, files or ChatGPT or Claude account details.
  • Use analytics, crash reporting, tracking pixels, advertising or cookies.
  • Sell, rent or share any data about you.
  • Use your data to train AI models or for any purpose other than saving the chats you asked to save.
  • Read other tabs, other websites, or your browsing history.

Payments, children, changes and contact

Email updates. If you sign up for Chat Preserver updates on this website, your email address is kept by Kit, our email service (Kit's privacy policy), and used only to send release notes and news about new features, a few times a year. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove you. We never share or sell the list, and we don't add buyers to it unless they sign up themselves. This is separate from the extension, which still collects nothing.

Payments. You buy Chat Preserver Pro inside the extension. The checkout is run by ExtensionPay, which keeps your email and whether you've paid so you can restore Pro on another computer (ExtensionPay's privacy policy). Stripe processes the card payment and collects your card details and billing address (Stripe's privacy policy). We see your name, email, country and what you bought so we can support you and handle refunds, and never your card number. We don't use them for marketing unless you ask us to.

Children. Chat Preserver is not directed at children under 13. The extension collects no information from anyone.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that would collect any data will be announced in the extension's store listing first.

Contact. Questions about privacy: support@chatpreserver.com.

Chat Preserver is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or Anthropic. Your use of ChatGPT and Claude is covered by OpenAI's and Anthropic's own terms and privacy policies.
