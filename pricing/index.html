Pro is bought inside the extension. Install Chat Preserver from the Chrome Web Store (or Edge Add-ons), click its toolbar button on ChatGPT or Claude, and choose Buy Pro. Checkout is by Stripe. Try it risk-free: full refund within 14 days, no questions asked.

Questions

How do I get Pro? In Chat Preserver, choose Buy Pro. A secure checkout page opens (ExtensionPay, paid by card through Stripe). When the payment goes through, Pro turns on right away. There is no key to copy.

I bought Pro for 2.x. Do I pay again for 3.0? No. Every 2.x purchase includes 3.0. If Pro ever shows as off, choose "Already bought? Restore purchase" with the email you paid with.

Does one purchase cover both ChatGPT and Claude? Yes. Pro is one license for both.

Will the price go up? Yes, once: the 3.0 launch price of $19.99 lasts until December 1, 2026, then Pro is $29.99 for new buyers. Anyone who has already paid keeps Pro.

New computer or reinstalled? Choose "Already bought? Restore purchase" and enter the email you paid with. You'll get a sign-in link, and Pro turns back on.

Does the Pro check send my chats anywhere? No. It only asks ExtensionPay whether this copy is paid. Your chats and files never leave your computer.

What if it doesn't work for me? You have 14 days to ask for a full refund. See the refund policy.

Prices and taxes. Prices are in US dollars. Where sales tax applies, it's added at checkout.
